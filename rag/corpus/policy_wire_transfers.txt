title: Wire Transfer Policy
access: public
---
Wire transfers may be initiated by verified account holders at any time via the MockBank Assistant or online banking.

Standard processing time for domestic wires is 1–2 business days. Wires submitted after the 4:00 PM ET cutoff are processed the next business day.

Transfers above $10,000 may require additional verification before release. International wires are not currently supported.

Domestic outgoing wires cost $25. Incoming wires are free.
