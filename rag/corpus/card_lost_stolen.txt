title: Lost or Stolen Cards
access: public
---
If your debit card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately in online banking or ask the assistant to connect you with card services. Freezing a card blocks new purchases but keeps recurring payments active.

Report the loss within 2 business days to limit your liability to $50. After 60 days from the statement date showing an unauthorised charge, MockBank may not be able to reimburse losses.

A replacement card arrives in 5–7 business days by standard delivery, or 2 business days by expedited delivery. The replacement has a new card number; update any merchants that store your card.
