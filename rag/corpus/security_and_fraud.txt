title: Security and Fraud Awareness
access: public
---
MockBank will never ask for your full password, one-time passcode, or PIN by phone, email, text or chat. Anyone asking for these is not MockBank.

Be wary of messages that create urgency, such as threats to close your account or requests to move money to a "safe account". MockBank never asks customers to move money to protect it.

If you think your account has been compromised, change your password, freeze your cards, and contact MockBank support. Review recent transactions and payees for anything you don't recognise.
