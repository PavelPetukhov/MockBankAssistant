title: Opening and Closing Accounts
access: public
---
Customers can open an additional checking or savings account online in about ten minutes. You need a verified MockBank profile and an initial deposit of at least $25.

Savings accounts earn 3.10% APY, variable, compounded daily and paid monthly.

To close an account, the balance must be zero or transferred to another account first. Accounts with pending transactions or open disputes cannot be closed until they are settled.
